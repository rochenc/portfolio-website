Automating enterprise application migration at scale

Intelligent automation platform for migrating enterprise applications to AWS with reduced manual effort and faster deployment cycles

Summary

I led the UX design for Migration Hub Automation, a platform that provides a library of automation units and the flexibility to create custom units. With this, migration customers, partners, and AWS practitioners have a centralized place for automation solutions ready to use for their migration projects—reducing repetitive work and minimizing errors in the process, speeding up and scaling the execution of migrations.

AWS Migration Hub

Migration Hub help customer migrate by providing tools to discover, assess, plan and execute migration tasks. It provides a central place for customer to track their migration resources and migration status across other AWS migration tools.

In addition to the AWS console experience, Migration Hub offers an outside-console experience for migration planning and collaboration between AWS practitioners, partners and customer .

Problem

Customer needs a streamlined experience that co-locates data needed and automates repetitive workflow to increase efficiency and experience.

Longitudinal Survey

To understand enterprise migration challenges, I conducted a longitudinal survey with 60 practitioners across the full migration lifecycle. Mapping their jobs-to-be-done across four key phases—Discover, Plan, Execute, and Observe—revealed a critical gap: Execute is the major pain point.

Jobs-to-be-Done: Importance vs Satisfaction

Key Insight: Execute planned migration efficiently is rated as high importance but low satisfaction, representing the primary opportunity for improvement through automation.

Follow-up Interviews

To understand the specific pain points around managing complex deployments. The research revealed two critical barriers that fragment the execution experience:

Siloed Tooling

With many migration tools from both AWS and 3rd-party partners, managing multiple platforms leads to broken workflows. Teams spend significant time switching contexts, replicating data, and coordinating across disconnected systems.

Access Management

Most customers work with AWS Partners or ProServe consultants. Sharing AWS accounts between internal teams and external partners is not a security best practice, creating access control complexities and compliance risks.

Final result

An intelligent, transparent automation platform that accelerates cloud migrations while maintaining human oversight and control throughout the entire process.

Outcome & Learnings

The platform has successfully reduced migration timelines by 60% on average, with teams reporting higher confidence in automated processes. Key learnings include the importance of progressive disclosure for complex operations, the value of smart error handling in reducing support burden, and how transparency in automation builds user trust. The experience taught me that successful automation isn't about removing the human from the process—it's about amplifying their capabilities through clear visibility and meaningful control.
