About

Ruobing Chen

I'm a product designer with a background in furniture and industrial design. Today I design tools that help people — and agents — work, communicate, and collaborate better.

Before this I designed subway-tiled shower surrounds for Kohler, improved the credit card experience for Citizens Bank, and made furniture inspired by Chinese philosophy. Some of it just whimsical.

How I work

I start with the user, the business, and the domain context, because in enterprise tooling the constraint usually is the domain. My work spans B2B tools across cloud services, logistics, finance and communications, plus high-touch consumer banking apps.

Focus

Strategic and vision design, user research and usability testing, interaction design and information architecture, data visualization, design systems, accessibility.
